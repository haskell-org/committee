# Grant Théophile "Hécate" Choutri maintainership rights to the www.haskell.org repo

As of our most recent Haskell.org committee election, Théophile
"Hécate" Choutri (@Kleidukos) is no longer a member of the committee,
and following policy
[`0004-haskell-org-repo-maintainers.md`](0004-haskell-org-repo-maintainers.md)
they should no longer have write access to the repositories associated
with the www.haskell.org website.  However, they are a valued
contributor to the community in general and to the www.haskell.org in
particular and the committee would like to permit them to continue to
contribute to the website.

This policy grants them the authority to have write access to the
repositories associated with the www.haskell.org website for the
purposes of maintaining the website, until the end of April 2029.
This authority is granted under the terms laid out for non committee
member maintainers in
[`0004-haskell-org-repo-maintainers.md`](0004-haskell-org-repo-maintainers.md).

Furthermore, when it comes to voting PRs to the www.haskell.org
website, their vote will count equal to the vote of a committee member
under the terms of [`0003-pr-process.md`](0003-pr-process.md).
