# Transfer authority over www.haskell.org to the Haskell Foundation

The Haskell.org committee has historically held responsibility over
the content and maintenance of the Haskell website at www.haskell.org.
both when it was the governing committee of Haskell.org, Inc. and as
delegated authority after the board members of Haskell Foundation,
Inc. became the board members of Haskell.org Inc, and the Haskell.org
committee became a subsidary committee.

After consideration, the Haskell.org committee believes that the best
way to run affairs related to the www.haskell.org website, including
seeking community contributions and keeping it fresh and up to date,
would be to transfer authority over it to the Haskell Foundation, to
take advantage of the processes the Haskell Foundation already has in
place to establish and maintain community involvement in important
projects.

As such, the Haskell.org committee hereby transfers authority over the
www.haskell.org website in perpetuity, subject to the Haskell
Foundation agreeing to receive such authority.
